## 1.0.1 - 2026-11-11

- Choose your settings with a first-run guide, or keep your current settings. The addon also shows release highlights in chat.
- Fix the options panel sometimes appearing empty when first opened.
- Spawn-location highlights now stay aligned when the world map is scaled.
- Yellow start pins for quests new in WoW Forever now use a yellow-to-blue gradient. You can turn the tint off in options.
- Updated the pinned QuestieDB Forever quest data.
- Holiday pins now appear only during their event window instead of for the whole year.
- AQ war effort pins wait until a war effort quest is offered or in your quest log; `/fqp wareffort always` overrides the phase check.
- Darkmoon Faire stays hidden on Forever until location-aware faire support is added.
